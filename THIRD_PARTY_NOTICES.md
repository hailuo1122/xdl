## Optional: ffmpeg (not bundled)

Users may optionally download a **LGPL** build of ffmpeg (e.g. BtbN FFmpeg Builds)
into `%LOCALAPPDATA%\XDL\bin`. ffmpeg runs as a separate process. Source and LGPL
text: https://ffmpeg.org/ and https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds.
